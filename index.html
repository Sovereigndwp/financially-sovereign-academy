Common questions

Is this really free? What's the catch?›

All 10 modules and the calculators are free: no signup, no email required, no advertising tracking. I (Dalia) build this because financial literacy is the cheapest leverage available to most adults. That does not change.

There are two optional things you can pay for, and neither is part of the course: an optional Lightning tip at the bottom of the page, and a small number of application kits for one specific situation each. The full explainer for every kit is published free on its own page before anything is for sale, so you can read it and walk away.

Do I need any background in finance?›

No. The modules are written for adults with no finance background. Math is explained as it appears.

How long will it take?›

4–6 hours of focused time, spread over 2–3 weeks if you do one or two modules per week. Faster if you binge.

Is this about Bitcoin?›

No. This is universal financial literacy. Bitcoin is mentioned in two places: (a) when it's relevant to a specific topic like inflation or storage of value, and (b) as an optional follow-up after Module 10 for readers who want to go further. The 10 modules don't assume or require any Bitcoin knowledge.

Will you sell me a course or financial product later?›

No course, no premium tier, no financial product, no affiliate links, and no commissions from anyone. The 10 modules, the calculators, and the assessment are free and stay free.

What does exist: a small number of application kits. Each one covers a single decision a household actually faces, and each is a set of printable worksheets, not information withheld from the free course. The explainer for every kit is published in full on its page, free, before anything is for sale. Every kit is refundable for 30 days, no questions asked.
