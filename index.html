Frequently asked questions

Is this really free?

Yes. All 10 core modules are 100% free forever. No credit card required, no signup, no paywall. We believe financial literacy should be a right, not a product.

Do I need any financial background?

None at all. The modules are designed from scratch for beginners. We explain every concept in plain English, build on prior modules, and use real-life scenarios throughout.

How long does it take to complete?

Most people complete all 10 modules in 4–6 hours, typically over 2–3 weeks at their own pace. Each module is 20–35 minutes. Use the progress tracker on this page to see where you are.

Is this about Bitcoin?

No. This is universal financial literacy — budgeting, debt, taxes, investing, insurance, and planning. Bitcoin appears as one tool among many where relevant. For Bitcoin-specific education, visit Bitcoin Sovereign Academy.

What's the catch?

There isn't one. This platform exists because financial education should be accessible to everyone. We offer paid institutional programs and consultations for those who want hands-on support, but the core education is and will always be free.

Will my progress be saved?

Yes — your progress is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. You can mark modules complete using the ✓ button on each card. Your progress persists between sessions on the same browser, no account required.
