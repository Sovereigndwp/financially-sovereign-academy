Common questions

Is this really free? What's the catch?

All 10 modules and the calculators are free, no signup, no email required, no advertising tracking. I (Dalia) build this because financial literacy is the cheapest leverage available to most adults. If you find it useful and want to support it, there's an optional Lightning tip address at the bottom of the page — that's the only ask, and it's optional.

Do I need any background in finance?

No. The modules are written for adults with no finance background. Math is explained as it appears.

How long will it take?

4–6 hours of focused time, spread over 2–3 weeks if you do one or two modules per week. Faster if you binge.

Is this about Bitcoin?

No. This is universal financial literacy. Bitcoin is mentioned in two places: (a) when it's relevant to a specific topic like inflation or storage of value, and (b) as an optional follow-up after Module 10 for readers who want to go further. The 10 modules don't assume or require any Bitcoin knowledge.

Will you sell me a course or financial product later?

No. There's no upsell. No course bundle. No "premium tier." No affiliate links. If that changes, this answer will change first.

Who's behind this?
