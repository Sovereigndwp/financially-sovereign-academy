Block 2 · Gate (new)

Is this module for you?

This module is for you if:

  • you have little or no savings right now,
  • a surprise bill would put you into debt, or
  • money comes in unevenly.

By the end you will: know your first cushion target, know where to keep it, and have a tracker to build it one step at a time.

Block 3 · Teaching

A cushion is a shock absorber, not a wealth plan

The first goal is not a big number. It is the first amount that changes what happens when something breaks. Even a small cushion changes your options in a bad week, because the most available money then is usually the most expensive.

Block 6 · Watch out for (standardized)

Watch out for

  • Keeping the cushion too easy to spend: keep it separate from daily money.
  • Treating a want as an emergency: write your own one-line definition first.
  • Using a credit card or payday loan as your "emergency fund": that is borrowing, not a cushion.
