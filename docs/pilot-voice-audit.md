# Voice Audit: Pilot Collection

Lightweight editorial pass, run after both articles cleared the six-stage pipeline.
Findings only. Nothing here is applied. A human approves any change. The educational
architecture is not in question; both articles teach well. This pass targets only the
few places where the writing sounds assembled rather than discovered.

## FSA-ARTICLE-000 "The Most Expensive Word is 'Later'"

This article exceeds the normal "small handful" threshold on one axis: repeated words.
That is itself the finding. In drafting I reached for a few motif words too often. Fixing
it is a focused de-tic pass, not a rewrite.

### 1. Repeated words (the main finding)
- **"quietly" x6.** Too many for one ~1,200-word piece. Keep the two that earn it and
  plainer verbs elsewhere. Keep: "'Later' quietly votes for the default." Reduce these:
  subtitle "quietly reshapes" -> "reshapes"; "quietly being made anyway" -> "being made
  anyway"; "has quietly collected about ninety-six dollars" -> "has collected about
  ninety-six dollars"; "quietly deciding for me right now" -> "deciding for me right now";
  closing "quietly already made" -> keep OR change (one of these last two keeps a "quietly").
- **"actually" x8.** Several are load-bearing ("what does 'later' actually cost"). Trim the
  filler ones: "if I never actually come back", "if I never actually decide", "the choice
  you are actually making", "until you actually understand" -> drop "actually" in 3 of 4.
- **"simply" x4 / "just" x8.** Trim filler: "The choice is simply parked" -> "The choice is
  parked"; "the money to act simply is not there" -> "the money to act is not there";
  "'Later' simply kept voting" -> "'Later' kept voting". Leave conversational "just" alone.

### 2. Over-engineered prose
- **"The un-compared policy renews at whatever price it defaults to."** "un-compared" is a
  manufactured word. Suggest: "The insurance you did not compare renews at whatever price it
  defaults to."
- **"delay changes the size of the number, not only its date."** The densest, most-assembled
  line (the compounding abstraction). It reads as constructed. The real fix is a companion
  calculator (see retrospective); as prose, consider loosening the "not only its date" balance.

### 3. Artificial balance
- **"It stops being 'Why am I so behind...' and becomes 'Which of my laters...'"** The
  "stops being X and becomes Y" frame. It is shared with the prototype (same section in both).
  Recommend varying ONE of the two so it is not a library-wide habit. Suggest varying it here.

### 4. Academic phrasing
- **"There is something real in this."** Shared verbatim with the prototype. Rephrase one of
  the two. Here: "There is something real in this." -> "Some of that is true."
- **"It also maps the rest of this library."** Reads like scaffolding. Suggest: "The rest of
  this library is really a closer look at one family of 'later' or another," folded into the
  next sentence.
- **Heading "What's actually happening."** Named as template-ish. This is a shared section
  label across the whole library, so changing it is a system-level decision, not a per-article
  edit. Flagged for your call; no change recommended unilaterally.

### 5. Observations to PROTECT (do not edit for consistency)
- "'Later' quietly votes for the default."
- "'Later' is not free. It quietly borrows from your future self, and your future self usually
  has less room to pay it back than you do right now."
- "The difference is between a wait you have chosen and a wait that just happens."
- "It went to a decision she never quite made."

## FSA-ARTICLE-001 "Why Saving Money Feels So Hard"

Clean. This is the 95%-leave-alone case. quietly x2, actually x1, simply x1. Only two flags.

### Flags
- **"It stops being 'How do I become the kind of person who saves?' and becomes 'How do I
  change the setup...'"** Same balanced frame as "Later." If we vary the instance in "Later"
  (above), this one can stand as the canonical use.
- **"There is something real in this."** Shared with "Later." Whichever article we leave it in,
  change the other. Recommend keeping it here (it reads naturally in this paragraph) and
  changing "Later."

### Observations to PROTECT
- "The money just found other work to do."
- "Today competes with tomorrow."
- "'just be disciplined' is asking willpower to do a job that design should be doing."
- "You are just moving the contest to more favorable ground."

## Cross-article note
Three items would become library-wide tics if left unaddressed: (a) "There is something real
in this," (b) the "it stops being X and becomes Y" frame in "Why this matters," (c) the
"What's actually happening" heading. Break (a) and (b) in one article each; treat (c) as a
system-level heading decision. Everything else is confined to "Later" and is a quick de-tic.

## Recommendation
Apply the "Later" word-trims and the three phrasing swaps; make one balance/phrase change in
"Why Saving" only if you want to break the shared frame there instead. Total: roughly a dozen
one-word deletions plus 3-4 short phrasing swaps in "Later," and 0-1 in "Why Saving." No
structural or educational change. Awaiting your go-ahead before touching the drafts.
