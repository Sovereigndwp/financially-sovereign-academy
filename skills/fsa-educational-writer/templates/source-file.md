**Type** = one of: `factual` (a specific number, rate, law, threshold), `research` (a
study or established finding), `regulatory` (an agency rule or official guidance).

**Preferred sources, in order:** government agencies; central banks where appropriate;
academic papers; established nonprofit educational institutions; official regulatory
materials; high-quality research organizations. Do not cite another educational article
because it explains a concept well; cite the underlying primary or authoritative source.

For U.S. personal-finance topics, common primary sources include: CFPB, FDIC, Federal
Reserve, FTC, IRS, Department of Labor, Department of Education, Social Security
Administration, and state regulators.
