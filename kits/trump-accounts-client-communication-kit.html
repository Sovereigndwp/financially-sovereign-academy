What "maintained" means

Your licence includes material updates for twelve months from purchase, at no additional cost. This is the part that matters right now, because the rules are still arriving.

  • A material change means a new instrument from the IRS, Treasury or the Department of Labor that changes what a client should actually do, or that makes a statement in the client material inaccurate. A proposed rule becoming final counts. Commentary, analysis and press coverage do not.
  • The commitment is a cadence, not a response time. Sources are reviewed weekly. Where a material change is found, a revised kit is issued and licensees are emailed. Where nothing has changed, nothing is sent.
  • Not included: personalised advice, review of your firm's own client materials, or answers about an individual client's situation.
