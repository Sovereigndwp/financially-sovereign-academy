02 Audience & positioning

Now grounded in one real buyer rather than inference alone.

Positioning is now evidence-backed, not inferred. The buyer who actually paid is a bilingual CPA practice serving families on both sides of the border. That is one observation, but it is the only one there is, and it points at the same segment the research pointed at: Spanish-serving practices whose clients ask questions no official material answers.

Positioning FSA can uniquely own

Banks, brokers and CPAs are publishing Trump Account explainers to sell their own products (529s, managed accounts, the 1–2% fee). FSA's edge is the opposite posture: first-principles, product-neutral money education that tells families the honest tradeoffs — including the parts the promoters skip (money is locked until 18; gains are taxed as ordinary income, not capital gains; "tax-deferred" is not "tax-free"). Add the bilingual + mixed-status treatment, including the cross-border gifting question no competitor answers, and the sovereignty frame ("who actually controls your child's account, and what are you trading away?"), and FSA owns a lane competitors structurally can't enter.

How it works

What it is. A custodial-style traditional IRA for minors, created under the 2025 "Working Families Tax Cuts" (OBBBA), new IRC §530A. Launched July 4, 2026. One account per child.

Opening it. File IRS Form 4547 with the 2025 return, or enroll online at TrumpAccounts.gov (app built with Robinhood). Enroll by the year before the child turns 18. No October-15 deadline exists — a widely repeated myth.

Who can fund it. Parents, guardians, grandparents, others — up to $5,000/yr combined, no income limit on contributors. Employers up to $2,500 (counts inside the $5k). A Dell Foundation program adds $250 charitable deposits for up to 25M kids ≤10 in qualifying ZIP codes.

The tax truth (the misinformation zone). Funds grow tax-deferred, not tax-free. No distributions during the growth period (except rollovers, excess-contribution, death). From Jan 1 of the age-18 year it's treated as a traditional IRA: contributions (after-tax) come out tax-free, but gains are taxed as ordinary income — losing the preferential capital-gains treatment a plain taxable account would get — and a 10% §72(t) early-withdrawal tax applies before 59½ unless an exception (qualified higher education, first home) applies.

Traction. Per the IRS, ~4M children signed up and ~1M have claimed the $1,000 pilot contribution.

Still pending / uncertain — flag on every asset: Update 21 Aug 2026: Treasury and IRS published proposed regulations on eligible investments (RIN 1545-BS14, Federal Register 21 Aug 2026, comments close 20 Oct 2026): index mutual funds or ETFs replicating an index, no leverage or derivatives materially increasing risk of loss, primarily US equity indexes under a 90% safe harbor, no sector-specific or ESG indexes, and total annual fees and expenses capped at 0.1% of net assets. Separately, Rev. Proc. 2026-25 (29 Jun 2026) gives a transfer-tax safe harbor treating cash contributions for a child under 18 as present-interest gifts eligible for the annual exclusion, removing a Form 709 filing requirement, subject to conditions. Final §530A regulations are still not published (proposed regs apply to tax years beginning on/after 1 Jan 2026; final regs intended within 18 months of enactment). Employer-contribution & nondiscrimination rules are proposed (REG-101355-26, under OMB review). DOL Technical Release 2026-02 says Trump Accounts generally aren't ERISA pension plans — but that too can evolve. State tax conformity varies. This uncertainty is the product: it is exactly why a 12-month "updates" subscription has real value.

The recurring engine

Decision, 24 Aug: do not build a subscription system. Make the existing product maintained rather than static, and include twelve months of updates in the $99. The purpose is to discover whether ongoing regulatory maintenance raises the product's value and makes partners more willing to distribute it. It is powered by the audit-claims workflow already in place, so the marginal cost is a weekly source review.

The terms are bounded, deliberately. A material change means a new IRS, Treasury or DOL instrument that changes what a client should actually do, or that makes a statement in the client material inaccurate; a proposed rule becoming final counts, commentary does not. The commitment is a review cadence, not a response time: sources reviewed weekly, revised kit issued and licensees emailed when something lands. Excluded: personalised advice, review of the firm's own materials, answers about an individual client. An open-ended promise is a liability, not a feature.

Funnel & distribution

06 The revised plan: three tracks, not five phases

v1 was five sequential phases and about twenty deliverables for one person. That is the shape that gets abandoned. Tracks run in parallel only because they do not compete for the same scarce resource, which is judgment and voice, not execution.

Why this parallelises honestly. The freshness audit discharged three obligations at once: it unblocked the firm-facing page, it produced the update owed to the founding licensee, and it was the first live proof that "maintained" means something. The gain is not doing more things at once. It is finding the work that settles several debts in one pass.
TRACK A · ONLY DALIA · ~1 HOUR PER WEEK

Conversations, judgment and relationships

  • Send the Latino Tax Pro approach. Ask how they normally get a resource in front of members before proposing any model.
  • Answer every reply personally. A reply is the highest-priority item in any week.
  • Deliver kit v2 to the founding licensee with a short note. This is the proof, not a courtesy.
  • Release the Finhabits approach if experiment one is silent after 14 days.

Cannot be delegated. Rule: one offer per contact, ever. The 51 contacted firms are closed.

TRACK B · CLAUDE BUILDS · DALIA APPROVES IN BATCHES

The distribution surface

  • Done: kit reissued as v2 against the 21 Aug regulations; firm-facing page written with bounded maintenance terms; sitemaps rebuilt for both domains.
  • Publish the firm page once v2 has reached the existing licensee.
  • Fix the calculator pages that carry no server-rendered explanation, which is the same defect that keeps them out of Google's index and out of AI answers.
  • Instrument: analytics on all three domains, offer IDs on every payment link.

Review, do not supervise. Nothing here needs her attention daily.

TRACK C · AUTOMATED · EVERY MONDAY

The part that remembers

  • Rebuilds campaign state from Gmail rather than from a spreadsheet, because the trackers lag reality and contact-form sends leave no trace.
  • Checks for new guidance weekly. This is the maintenance promise, enforced.
  • Drafts the next finished email into the drafts folder. One first touch per week, never a batch.
  • Counts down to 20 October and reports days remaining.
  • Refuses new product work during the freeze and names what it would displace.

Output: under 200 words. A quiet week is three lines plus one draft.
