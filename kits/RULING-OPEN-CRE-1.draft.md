# Owner ruling — OPEN-CRE-1

**Question:** Are 12-month updates to a purchased kit a *forbidden subscription to information*
(retired by owner decision 2026-06-17), or *permitted maintenance of an application layer*?

**Why this is blocking:** `METRICS.md` §3, FM-4 — *"OPEN-CRE-1 unresolved while an updates SKU is
promoted → any promotion before the ruling → stop."* The Trump Accounts firm licence page promotes
twelve months of material updates. It does not publish until this is ruled.

**Status:** DRAFT — prepared for the owner 2026-08-24. Not in force until signed and dated below.

---

## The case for "permitted"

1. `OFFER-MAP.md` §1 already lists `maintenance` as a permitted `value_source`:
   *"Keeping an application layer current as rules change."* The forbidden value source is
   `information`, and it is forbidden precisely because information should be free.
2. What the licensee receives is not access to knowledge. The underlying explainer is published
   free in both languages and stays free. What is maintained is the **brandable, distributable,
   source-mapped artifact** — the assembly work, not the understanding.
3. The 2026-06-17 retirement was of *"Membership / paid access to core education."* Core education
   is not what is being maintained here.
4. The subject matter genuinely moves. Two sets of proposed regulations landed within eleven days
   in August 2026. A static file on this topic decays into a liability for the firm distributing it.

## The case for "forbidden"

1. A recurring charge for "we will tell you when something changes" is a subscription to
   information wearing a maintenance costume. The test is whether the buyer would still pay if
   the underlying facts never changed.
2. It creates a standing incentive to find changes worth announcing — which is how a trust surplus
   gets spent on the wrong ask (FM-1).

## Proposed ruling

**PERMITTED, bounded, and non-recurring in this form**, subject to all five conditions:

1. **Not a recurring charge.** Twelve months of updates are included in the one-time price.
   No renewal, no auto-billing, no lapse-and-repurchase. If a recurring SKU is ever proposed, it
   is a new question and this ruling does not cover it.
2. **A material change is defined narrowly and in public.** A new instrument from the IRS,
   Treasury or DOL that changes what a client should do, or that makes a statement in the client
   material inaccurate. A proposed rule becoming final counts. Commentary, analysis and press
   coverage do not.
3. **The commitment is a cadence, not a response time.** Sources reviewed weekly. Where a material
   change is found, a revised kit is issued. Where nothing has changed, nothing is sent — and
   sending nothing is the expected outcome in most weeks.
4. **The free layer is maintained on the same cadence, at the same time.** If the paid kit is
   corrected and the free guide is not, this ruling is violated and the offer stops. This is the
   condition that keeps it maintenance rather than a paywall on currency.
5. **Instrumented against FM-1.** Refund rate and unsubscribes within 48h of any update email are
   logged. Refunds above 10% or an unsubscribe spike stops the offer pending review.

## What this ruling does not authorise

Any recurring subscription; any updates SKU sold separately from the artifact; any promotion of
"updates" as the primary value of the offer; extending this reasoning to any other SKU without
its own ruling.
