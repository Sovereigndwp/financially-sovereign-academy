## 5. Myth vs fact

Cross out the myth once you have read the fact.

| Myth | Fact |
|---|---|
| Carrying a balance builds credit | Paying in full and on time is what helps |
| Checking my own score hurts it | Checking your own score does not lower it |
| Closing old cards helps my score | Closing them can shorten history and raise utilization |
