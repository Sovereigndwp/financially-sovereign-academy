## 4. W-4 review steps

Your W-4 tells your employer how much to withhold. Review it after big life changes.

- [ ] Confirm my filing status is current
- [ ] Note any second job or my partner's job
- [ ] Note dependents, if any
- [ ] Check for any extra withholding I set before
- [ ] Use the IRS withholding estimator for the current year if unsure
- [ ] Ask HR how to submit an updated W-4
