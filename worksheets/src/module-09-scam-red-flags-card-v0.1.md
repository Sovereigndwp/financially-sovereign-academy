# Scam Red-Flags Wallet Card

**Financially Sovereign Academy · Module 9: Consumer Protection · Worksheet v0.1 · 2026-07-04**

**What this is.** Scams work by rushing you and scaring you. This one page is a card you can cut out and a checklist for if it happens to you. Fill in your local contacts by hand. Example amounts are examples only; use your own numbers.

---

## 1. Red-flags wallet card (cut out and keep)

From Module 9: if you see these, stop and slow down.

- [ ] Pressure to act right now or lose out
- [ ] Asking for gift cards, wire transfers, or crypto to "fix" a problem
- [ ] A stranger who knows your name and claims to be your bank or the government
- [ ] "You won" something you never entered
- [ ] Requests to keep it secret from family or your bank
- [ ] A payment method with no way to get money back

My one rule to remember: real institutions do not rush me or forbid me to check. When in doubt, hang up and call back on a number I look up myself.

## 2. If I have been scammed: action checklist

Do these as soon as you can. Acting fast can help.

- [ ] Stop all contact with the person
- [ ] Call my bank or card company to report and possibly freeze
- [ ] Change passwords on any account they touched
- [ ] Save messages, receipts, and phone numbers as proof
- [ ] Write down what happened while it is fresh
- [ ] Report it to the places below
