## 2. Pick your order

From Module 5, there are two common orders. Neither is "correct" for everyone.

- **Avalanche:** pay extra on the highest APR first. Saves the most on interest.
- **Snowball:** pay extra on the smallest balance first. Gives quick wins for motivation.
