# One-Week Cash-Flow Map

**Financially Sovereign Academy · Module 1: Money Mindset & Cash Flow · Worksheet v0.1 · 2026-07-04**

**What this is.** A cash-flow map shows where your money went this week, not where you wish it went. This one page helps you see money in, money out, and the one number left over. Fill it in by hand. Example amounts are examples only; use your own numbers.
