Free tools

Working tools, not lead magnets. Each one does arithmetic or sorting on numbers you type in, shows you the result, and then tells you which question it cannot answer for you.

What these tools do not do. They collect nothing. Nothing you type leaves your browser — there is no account, no tracking, no email gate, and no result is stored or sent anywhere. Every figure is linked to the primary source it comes from, with that source's date. Where a rule is still being written, the tool says so instead of rounding it off.
