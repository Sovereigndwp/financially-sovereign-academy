The Financial Literacy Challenge You Face

Understanding the specific pressure points helps us design the right educational program for your organization.

⚠️ Hidden Productivity Tax

Employees with high financial stress are 3x more likely to be distracted at work, miss deadlines, and take unplanned absences. The productivity cost is real — and invisible in most HR dashboards.

🚪 Retention and Recruiting Pressure

Top talent increasingly evaluates employers based on financial wellness benefits. Companies without structured financial education programs are losing candidates and experienced employees to competitors who offer them.

🏥 Rising Healthcare Costs from Financial Stress

Financial stress is directly linked to elevated cortisol, hypertension, and mental health claims — all of which drive up employer healthcare costs. Financial wellness is preventive health care.

📊 Financial Benefits Left on the Table

Most employees don't maximize 401k matches, HSA contributions, and equity compensation plans — leaving thousands in annual compensation unclaimed. Proper education captures this value for both employee and employer.
