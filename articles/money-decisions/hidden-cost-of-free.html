The Hidden Cost of “Free”

Editorial brief

Examines what 'free' actually means in money terms, free checking, free trials, free financing. The point is not that free is a trap, but that the price simply moved: to your time, your attention, your data, or a later bill. Teaches learners to ask where the cost went instead of assuming it vanished.

Core question
If no money changes hands, why does ‘free’ still cost something?
Mental model
Free usually changes who pays, when they pay, or what they give up.
Learner outcome
The learner can identify the non-money cost hidden inside a 'free' offer before accepting it.
Supporting concepts
incentives, opportunity cost, attention
