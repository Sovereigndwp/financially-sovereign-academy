Incentives Explain More Than You Think

Editorial brief

Introduces incentives as a lens: much of what looks like malice or stupidity is people responding to what they're rewarded for. Applies it to fees, sales advice, and free offers so learners can predict behavior and read a situation before trusting it, without becoming cynical about everyone.

Core question
When a system produces a bad outcome, where should you look first?
Mental model
Follow the incentive before you judge the person.
Learner outcome
The learner can ask who is rewarded for what before accepting advice or a product.

motivation, conflict of interest, design
