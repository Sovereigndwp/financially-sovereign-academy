Your Financial Life Is a Series of Tradeoffs

Editorial brief

Reframes money decisions as choosing between downsides rather than searching for a flawless option. Shows that naming the tradeoff, what each path costs you, leads to clearer decisions than chasing a 'right answer,' and that different people can rationally choose differently.

Core question
What if there is no option without a downside, only different downsides?
Mental model
Every choice closes another door.
Learner outcome
The learner can name what is given up in a decision, not only what is gained.
Supporting concepts
opportunity cost, priorities, constraints
