The Most Expensive Word in Personal Finance: “Later”

How a small, sensible-sounding word quietly reshapes a financial life.

Editorial brief

An introduction to the whole library. Most money problems do not arrive as dramatic mistakes; they arrive as a series of reasonable-sounding postponements. This piece names the pattern, the small, respectable word 'later', and shows how delay changes who pays and how much, without shaming anyone for using it. It sets up the four series that follow.

Core question
What does it actually cost to postpone a money decision?
Mental model
“Later” is not free; it quietly borrows from your future self.
Learner outcome
The learner can notice when “later” is doing the work of a decision, and ask what the delay costs.
