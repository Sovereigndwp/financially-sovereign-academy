How Inflation Changes Your Choices

Editorial brief

Moves inflation from a headline number to a lived experience: the quiet erosion of what a fixed amount buys, and how it reshapes decisions about holding cash, borrowing, and saving. Uses dated, sourced figures and, where relevant, notes how Bitcoin enters this conversation as one response among several, without endorsing it as a guaranteed answer.

Core question
If your account balance is the same, how can you be poorer a year later?
Mental model
Inflation doesn’t change your number, it changes what your number buys.
Learner outcome
The learner can think in purchasing power, not just dollar amounts, when making money decisions.
Supporting concepts
purchasing power, real vs nominal, cash
