Good Debt vs. Bad Debt Isn’t About the Loan

Editorial brief

Challenges the neat 'mortgage good, credit card bad' framing. Argues the same loan type can be either, depending on rate, purpose, and whether it expands or shrinks your future options. Gives learners a way to judge a debt in front of them rather than sorting by category.

Core question
What actually makes one debt helpful and another harmful?
Mental model
Debt is good or bad based on what it does for you, not what it’s called.
Learner outcome
The learner can evaluate a specific debt by its terms, purpose, and effect on their options, not its label.
