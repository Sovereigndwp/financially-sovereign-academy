Income Solves Different Problems Than Wealth

Editorial brief

Distinguishes the flow (income) from the stock (wealth) and shows they solve different problems: income covers recurring needs, wealth provides resilience and choice. Explains why the two can move independently and why building one doesn't automatically build the other.

Core question
Why can a high earner feel financially fragile while a modest saver feels secure?
Mental model
Income pays this month’s problems; wealth buys options for the future.
Learner outcome
The learner can tell which of their goals need more income and which need accumulated wealth.
Supporting concepts
cash flow, assets, security
