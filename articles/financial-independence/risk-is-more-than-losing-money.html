Risk Is More Than Losing Money

Editorial brief

Broadens 'risk' beyond the chance of a dollar loss to include the risk of needing money at the wrong moment, the risk of not recovering, and the risk of doing nothing while inflation bites. Helps learners match the kind of risk they're actually exposed to with the response that fits.

Core question
Is the bigger risk losing money, or running out of it at the wrong time?
Mental model
Real risk is not being able to recover, not just going down.
Learner outcome
The learner can weigh several kinds of risk, loss, timing, and inability to recover, instead of only one.
Supporting concepts
volatility, ruin, time horizon
