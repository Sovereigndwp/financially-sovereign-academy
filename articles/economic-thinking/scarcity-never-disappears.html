Scarcity Never Disappears

Editorial brief

Separates two things people blur. Material deprivation is not having enough for real needs, and more income genuinely can remove painful shortages and expand real freedom; the article says this plainly and does not romanticize going without. Economic scarcity is different: money, time, and attention stay limited at every income, so choosing among them never fully ends. The article makes the distinction clearly, so a reader neither feels that scarcity is a personal failing nor hears the false comfort that a raise erases every hard choice.

Core question
What does more money actually remove, and what stays no matter what?
Mental model
More money can end a shortage, but not the need to choose.
Learner outcome
The learner can tell material deprivation, which more income can relieve, apart from economic scarcity, the permanent need to choose among limited money, time, and attention.
