Sunk Costs Trap Smart People

Editorial brief

Explains the sunk cost trap, letting unrecoverable past spending drive future decisions, and why it snares careful, committed people especially. Gives learners a clean test: decide from where you are now, not from what you've already paid, while acknowledging the emotional pull that makes this hard.

Core question
Why is it so hard to walk away from something you’ve already put money into?
Mental model
Money already spent is gone; only what happens next is your decision.
Learner outcome
The learner can make a forward-looking choice without being anchored by past spending.
Supporting concepts
loss aversion, commitment, decision-making
