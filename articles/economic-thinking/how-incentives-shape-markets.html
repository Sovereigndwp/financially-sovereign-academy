How Incentives Shape Markets

Editorial brief

Connects the series' ideas at the level of markets: how countless individual incentives, transmitted through prices, coordinate behavior no one is centrally directing. Gives learners a mental model for why prices move and what they reveal, staying practical rather than ideological.

Core question
If no one is in charge of prices, what makes them move?
Mental model
Prices are signals; incentives are the current underneath them.
Learner outcome
The learner can read a price change as information about incentives and scarcity, not just a number.
Supporting concepts
prices as signals, competition, unintended effects
