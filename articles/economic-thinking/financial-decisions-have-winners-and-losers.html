Every Financial Decision Has Winners and Losers

Editorial brief

Teaches learners to map the winners and losers inside any financial arrangement, a rewards card, a subsidy, a 'free' service, because gains and costs usually land on different people. Sharpens judgment about deals and advice by asking who is on each side, without assuming bad intent.

Core question
When something is 'a great deal,' a great deal for whom?
Mental model
Ask who gains and who pays, the answer is rarely 'everyone.'
Learner outcome
The learner can identify who benefits and who bears the cost in a financial arrangement.
Supporting concepts
who benefits, second-order effects, fees
