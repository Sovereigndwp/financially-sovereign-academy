**Note on the "spending is engineered to be smooth" observation.** The article's point that
modern payment design (saved cards, one-tap checkout) reduces the friction of spending is a
general, widely observable statement, not a specific statistic. It is presented as an
observation, not cited as evidence. The Thaler & Benartzi source above supports the paired,
actionable claim that reducing friction on the saving side raises saving.
