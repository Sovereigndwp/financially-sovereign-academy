**Note on the "the world does not pause" observation.** The article's point that a parked
decision keeps running (a subscription keeps charging, a balance keeps growing, a policy
renews at its default) is a general, widely observable statement about how automatic
processes work, not a specific statistic. It is presented as an observation, not cited as
evidence.
