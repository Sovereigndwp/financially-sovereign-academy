Why Emergencies Become Financial Emergencies

Editorial brief

Shows how an ordinary event (a car repair, a missed shift) becomes a financial emergency through timing and cascading late fees, not just low income. Names the mechanism, no cash on hand at the moment of the shock, and why even a small buffer changes the outcome, while acknowledging that building one is genuinely hard on a tight budget.

Core question
Why can the same $400 surprise be a shrug for one household and a spiral for another?
Mental model
It’s rarely the size of the shock, it’s whether cash is there when it hits.
Learner outcome
The learner can distinguish an income problem from a timing problem, and see how small buffers stop cascades.
Supporting concepts
cash buffer, timing, cascading costs
