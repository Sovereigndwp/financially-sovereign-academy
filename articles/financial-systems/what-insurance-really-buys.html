What Insurance Really Buys

Editorial brief

Reframes insurance from 'a bet you usually lose' to 'transferring the rare, ruinous loss to a pool.' Clarifies that the goal is not to come out ahead on average but to survive the low-probability, high-damage event, and that some small risks are better self-insured.

Core question
If you usually 'lose' on insurance, why buy it at all?
Mental model
Insurance buys protection from the loss you can’t recover from, not a return.
Learner outcome
The learner can decide which risks are worth insuring and which are cheaper to carry.
Supporting concepts
pooling, catastrophic loss, peace of mind
