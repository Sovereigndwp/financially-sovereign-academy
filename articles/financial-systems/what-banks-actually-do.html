What Banks Actually Do

Editorial brief

Explains what a bank does with deposits, connecting savers and borrowers, running the payment rails, and keeping enough on hand for withdrawals, and why deposit insurance exists. Aimed at learners who use banks daily but were never told how they work, without drifting into ideology.

Core question
If a bank lends out deposits, where is your money when you’re not using it?
Mental model
A bank is a hub that moves money between savers and borrowers, not a vault.
Learner outcome
The learner can explain, in plain terms, how a bank uses deposits and why that matters to them.
Supporting concepts
deposits, lending, trust
