Why Credit Exists

Editorial brief

Explains the underlying purpose of credit, letting people use future income today when timing, not total resources, is the constraint, and why lenders price it by risk. Neither demonizes nor romanticizes borrowing; helps learners tell a useful use of credit from a costly one.

Core question
What problem is credit actually trying to solve?
Mental model
Credit moves future income into the present, for a price.
Learner outcome
The learner can see credit as a tool that trades future money for present money, and judge when that trade helps or hurts.
Supporting concepts
trust, future income, risk pricing
